Our Mission & Story

Founded in 2008, Acme Home Services started as a family-owned plumbing shop in Chicago. Over the past 18 years, we've grown into Chicagoland's premier home repair network, expanding into HVAC and Electrical services while retaining our commitment to local families.

We believe in upfront flat-rate pricing without hidden fees or surprise billings. Every technician on our team is background-checked, drug-tested, and fully certified.
